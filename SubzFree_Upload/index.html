Frequently Asked Questions

Is SubzFree really free?
Yes, 100%. You use all features — AI captions, 60+ styles, 4K export — completely free. When you click Download, you watch a 30-second ad. That ad revenue is how we pay for everything. You never pay money.
Does my video get uploaded to a server?
No. The video rendering and caption burning happens entirely in your browser using Canvas API — your video file never leaves your device. For transcription, only the audio is sent to the AI service, not the video.
How accurate is the Hinglish transcription?
We use AI models with strong support for code-mixed Hinglish — handling "yaar", "bhai", mixed sentences, and most Indian accents. You can always fix any word in the word-level editor before exporting. If accuracy is critical, use the "Paste Script" mode to sync your own lyrics perfectly.
Which platforms can I export for?
9:16 vertical for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and TikTok. 16:9 horizontal for YouTube and Facebook. Square 1:1 for Instagram posts. All resolutions up to 4K.
Will SubzFree stay free forever?
Yes. Our entire business model is ad monetization — we earn more when more people use the tool. We have zero incentive to add a paywall. The more free users we have, the more ads we show, the more we earn. It's a win-win.
